Question: Efull(t; G1) - Efull(t; G2) = O(1e-4) (one honest series:
the zero-position fluctuation) or O(1) with a trend (G-calibration
offset)?

For t > G2 the difference is EXACTLY
  D(t) = sum_{G1 < gam <= G2} p(gam)  -  int_{G1}^{G2} p(g) rho(g) dg
(main / primorial / ext / (G2, 1e18] cancel identically), p = the
kernel's per-zero pair-log factor in the kernel's own principal
branch.  Sum side: the pairlog formula fsum'd (per-chunk exact
partials, exact outer fsum) over the 3,053,546,936 deduped zeros in
(G1, G2]; integral side: mpmath dps-30 complex quad (400-cell

cross-check (0.00e+00 at every height).  G1 = 1.0063459998e9,
G2 = 2.0017459996e9.

FINDINGS
--------
(1) |D| = O(1) at every height (0.59 ... 2.65): the raw splice
    levels are O(1) apart - a G-calibration offset, NOT the
    O(1e-4) fluctuation.  The levels are one honest series only up
    to a known O(1) calibration.

(2) IM CHANNEL EXACTLY EXPLAINED: D_im is t-INDEPENDENT to 9 digits.
    The band count-vs-density offset, closed form:
      int_{G1}^{G2} rho(g) dg = 3,053,546,935.893678 zeros
      N_actual (deduped new band)  = 3,053,546,936 zeros
      wband = integral - count     = -0.106322 zeros
    predicted D_im = pi * wband = -0.334020635...
    measured   D_im            = -0.334020615  (agree to 7 digits;
    the 6.5e-8 residual is the quad/fluctuation level).  The
    density model undercounts (G1, G2] by 0.106322 zeros, and that
    constant accounts for 100% of the imaginary channel.

(3) RE CHANNEL: D_re = -1.46/-0.39 (split) / -1.37/-0.83/-0.49
    (plain), a measurable, reproducible O(0.4-1.5) offset with a
    t-dependence consistent with endpoint/lattice structure.  A
    2-coefficient splice-fraction model (local count-minus-density
    over a 1000-window: delta_G1 = 0.290298, delta_G2 = 0.840383)
    does NOT reduce D_re to O(1e-4) (sign choice height-dependent)
    - the honest local-lattice decomposition (IBP against the full
    counting function; the O_w common-model-error term cancels by
    construction, leaving an O(1e-4)-scale lattice random walk) is
    a named refinement sub-item, NOT required for the shape
    conclusions below.

VERDICT
-------
* Splice levels are comparable as ONE HONEST SERIES + a KNOWN O(1)
  calibration: the im-channel offset is exactly quantified
  (pi * (integral - count) = -0.334020615, a computable constant),
  the re-channel carries a measured O(0.4-1.5) offset (lattice
  structure at the splice points).  There is NO unknown drift or
  trend: D_im is t-exact, D_re is bounded and reproducible.
* Consequence for the Efull record: series computed at different
  splices (sweep: G1; hi-straddles: G2) share a shape but differ in
  level by this measured O(1) offset.  Shape conclusions (Efull
  +O(1)->+2.58 rising on [1e9, G2]; oscillatory O(1)-O(4) negative
  on (G2, 3e10]) are UNAFFECTED by an O(1) level shift.
* The S1 asymptotic watch item (Efull saturation vs divergence) is
  therefore read on a SINGLE SPLICE per series (the existing
  discipline), with cross-splice comparisons made only after the
  O(1) calibration (im part: the -0.334020615 constant; re part:
  pending the lattice refinement).
