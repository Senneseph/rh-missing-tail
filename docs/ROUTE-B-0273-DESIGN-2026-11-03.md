## 2. The non-circular argument for the central band (0273 core)

For a hypothetical off-line zero ρ in the central band, the Weil explicit
formula with a test function ψ supported in D(ρ, r) gives

      0 = log ζ(ρ) [by hypothesis]
        = T_self(ψ; ρ) + T_known(ψ) + T_primes(ψ; ρ) + T_other(ψ)

where
  T_self    — the ρ-term of the zero sum: scale = the 0272 removable
              residue, |T_self| ≤ |Dζ(ρ)|·‖ψ‖ ≤ (Cauchy)
              (max_{∂D(ρ,r)} |ζ|)/r · ‖ψ‖ ≤ (PL bound t^{(1−σ')/2}
              (log t)^{5/6} e^{c/log t})/r · ‖ψ‖  — UNIVERSAL (no
              knowledge of ρ or ζ'(ρ) needed);
  T_known   — the zero sum over the KNOWN zeros below tPT2 (bit-pinned
              Platt record, 0263): EXACT. Efull-splice evaluation:
              discrete sum to t − W + density quad beyond, splice error
              bounded by the exact in-band zero counts (the S statistic
              is known below tPT2);
  T_primes  — explicit prime-power sum (absolutely convergent for Re ρ >
              0 with this ψ): computable to ANY precision;
  T_other   — the unknown zeros ≠ ρ (heights ≥ tPT2): bounded by
              R-vM (disk argument: N(t, t+1) ≤ A·log t, Titchmarsh 9.2,
              as in the W2-LEAN-PLAN cited Zeta23-RvM result) times the
              per-zero decay ‖ψ‖·1/|t − γ| — a log W bound.

  EXCLUSION: if  |T_known| + |T_primes| − |T_other-bounded|  >  |T_self-bounded|
  (a lower bound on the "other-sides" magnitude exceeding the universal
  upper bound of the self-term), then 0 = T_self + (rest) is IMPOSSIBLE —
  ρ is not a zero. No knowledge of ρ's derivative, no knowledge of the
  unknown zero positions beyond R-vM — NON-CIRCULAR.

  LIVER: the test function ψ is a free parameter (per ρ). The pointwise
  oscillatory sums T_known + T_primes pass through 0 (random-cancellation
  worst case), but sup_ψ |T_known(ψ) + T_primes(ψ;ρ)| over an admissible
  family (phase, frequency, width) is a genuine degree of freedom: the
  exclusion is sup over ψ of [other-side lower bound] vs [self bound].
  The probe measures this sup structure first at accessible heights.

## 3b. Verified identity (2026-11-03, after the xi-product test)

The probe's identity, verified against the direct xi product at
s = 1/2+50i to ~1e-2 (the tail):

      log|zeta(s)| = log(1/2) - Re log[0.5 s (s-1) pi^{-s/2} Gamma(s/2)]
                     + SUM_{g > 0}  f(g; s)

  f(g;s) = [log|1-s/r| + Re(s/r)] + [log|1-s/rb| + Re(s/rb)],
           r = 1/2 + i g,  rb = 1/2 - i g.

LESSONS (both cost a failed first pass):
  1. The genus-1 E1-Re terms are MANDATORY for convergence: the
     separate sums  SUM log|1-s/r|  and  SUM Re(s/r)  each DIVERGE
     (log-scale) and cancel only in combination.  A "pair product"
     form without the Re terms is ~1000 off (measured).
  2. Explicit real form (what the probe computes): with a = 1/2 - sr
     (s = sr + i t),
       f(g;s) = 0.5 ln(((a^2 + (g-t)^2) + ... ))  i.e.
       f = 0.5 ln((a^2 + (g-t)^2)/(0.25+g^2))
         + 0.5 ln((a^2 + (g+t)^2)/(0.25+g^2)) + sr/(0.25+g^2).
     numpy-float64 vs mpmath-dps40: agree to 3e-4 at the dip (the
     float64 ulp of t at 3e12; consistent since the data zeros are
     float64).

DIP STRUCTURE (new): f has a sharp dip at g = Im(s): for sr < 1/2,
|1 - s/r| -> |a|/|t| = (1/2-sr)/t at g = t, so
f(t; s) ~ -2 ln(t/a) + 2 ln 2 ~ -(2 ln(t/a)) (e.g. -32.7 at
sr = 0.49, t = tPT2; -29.2 at sr = a = 0.1252270103).  The dip is
O(1)-wide in g (width ~ a = 1/2-sr), so it contains only
O(density) ~ 5-10 KNOWN-geometry zeros - the fluctuation there is
bounded by O(10 * depth), not by the window count.  This is why the
integration-by-parts bound
  |SUM f dN - Int f rho dg| <= B1 * (|f(g1)| + |f(t2)| + TV(f)),
  B1 = A0 log(tPT2)  (Titchmarsh 9.2 count error)
STAYS small (O(1e4)) even though the smooth f-quad is O(1e13): the
oscillation is count-weighted, not window-count-weighted.

The bound is VALID only if  |f| and TV(f) are computed with the dip
resolved (dense linear breakpoints at g = t +/- 200) - a log-only
grid under-resolves the dip and under-bounds.
