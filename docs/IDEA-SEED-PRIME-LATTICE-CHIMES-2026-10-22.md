## 1. The idea, in the owner's own words

> "…each time a prime linked prime grows in the sieve to have a prime
> number of members (for example 2x3, 2x5, 2x7, it is prime to 3 at
> integer 7) then the LATTICE CHIMES and we hit a recursive revisitation
> of prime relatinships and since this is an isomorphic event at every
> new prime connection level, then like the next level must hit new areas
> that have a uniform characteristic, like if we took all our matched
> leaf triangles (each forming two parts of a heart), we can test this by
> finding if the running total of areas of the conjugation of primes at
> any given level gives a compass to the area we must traverse before the
> next prime, that is the metric - can it predict the next prime then we
> have found a pattern that matters and we might master though it might
> never have been characterized."

## 2. Faithful reconstruction (for the executing session)

Objects:
- **Sieve-growth member count of a linked prime.** Each prime branch
  (the seed's "prime linked prime") grows members as the sieve runs —
  e.g. the branch generated by 2 acquires members 2×3, 2×5, 2×7, …
  ("2x3, 2x5, 2x7": the first members of the 2-branch are the products
  2·p over successive primes p; "it is prime to 3 at integer 7" = the
  member count 7 is itself prime when the branch reaches 7 members).
- **The CHIME.** Whenever a branch's member count becomes a PRIME
  NUMBER, the lattice "chimes": mark the event (which branch, which
  count, at what height / stage of the sieve). Chimes are thus
  arithmetic events: c(p-branche) ∈ primes.
- **Recursive revisitation.** The chime event triggers a "recursive
  revisitation of prime relationships": re-examine the prime
  lattice structure at that stage. The claim to test: **chime events
  are ISOMORPHIC at every new prime-connection level** — the pattern of
  revisited relationships at level n+1 has the same form as at level n,
  only applied to a new (fresh) area of the lattice with a uniform
  characteristic.
- **Matched leaf triangles / hearts.** From pass 1: the matched
  triads/leaf-triangles — each leaf-triangle, paired with its conjugate
  leaf, "forms two parts of a heart" (a mirror pair sharing an axis).
  Conjugation here = the natural mirror: triad (p, p+d, p+2d) ↔ its
  reflected / complementary triad (readings to pin down at execution:
  reflection d ↔ −d about the triad's midpoint, or the F6-conjugate
  pairing (p, q) ↔ complement in the same AP-window — the executor
  should try both and note which produces the matching motif).
- **THE METRIC (the actual testable claim).** Take all matched
  leaf-triangles at a given level; compute the **running total of the
  "areas of the conjugation of primes"** at that level (area = a
  concrete measure of a leaf-pair's extent — e.g. the base×height
  measure of the triangle pair on the prime axis, or the
  (p+d)·(p−3d) square-defect product from F2 as the "area"
  candidate — executor to define and freeze ONE concrete area before
  fitting). Then: **does the running total, read at level n, act as a
  COMPASS for "the area we must traverse before the next prime"** —
  i.e. does the metric at the last chime predict the GAP TO THE NEXT
  PRIME?
- **Success criterion (explicit).** The metric predicts the next prime
  (its location, to better-than-random accuracy over a held-out prime
  set: e.g. correlation of predicted gap vs actual gap > what the
  trivial li-prediction gives, or a measurable improvement in
  next-prime localization over the prime-density baseline). If it can:
  "a pattern that matters" — possibly never-before-characterized
  structure in the prime gaps, sitting at the chime levels. If not:
  the chime levels are documented as an isomorphism curiosity of the
  sieve-growth process, and the idea files next to the seed.

## 3. Concrete test protocol for the execution pass

1. **Grow the sieve by branches** up to X (reuse the 2e6 table):
   for each prime p ≤ X, branch B_p = {p·m : m prime, p·m ≤ X}
   (and, as variant, the AP-branch from pass 1: B'_p = primes q such
   that (p, q, r) or (r, p, q) is a joinder pair — the pass-1 graph).
   Record each branch's member count over the growth order (order =
   increasing member value).
2. **Chime census.** All (p, k) with k-th member of B_p and k prime:
   count, joint distribution of (size of p, k), first chimes per branch.
   Test the isomorphism claim: chime-spacing statistics at branch level
   1 (p small) vs level 2 (p large) vs level 3 (p > X/2) — same
   distribution? (normalized chime-density vs branch size.)
3. **Leaf/heart matching.** Freeze one conjugation (candidate (a):
   midpoint mirror within the AP window; candidate (b): the F6
   Fourier-conjugate pairing of the deficit spectrum — the owner's
   "conjugation of primes" most naturally = (a) in the lattice, (b)
   in the FANTASY layer). For each matched pair, define ONE area
   measure (candidate: defect product |p² + q² − r²| from F2, or
   geometric base·height on the prime axis) and the running total at
   each chime level.
4. **Compass test.** At chime i (last chime before prime p_{n+1} in
   growth order), read the metric M_i; compare M_i against the actual
   gap g_{n+1} = p_{n+1} − p_n over a held-out range. Baselines:
   (i) trivial: predict E[gap | height] = ln p; (ii) li-residual:
   the FANTASY zero-sum local prediction (F6/F7 machinery). Metric
   beats (i) AND (ii) by a measurable margin → the owner's success
   criterion met → new object. Otherwise → document and file.
5. **Record.** Whatever the verdict: chime census tables, the
   isomorphism test result, the concrete area used, the compass
   correlation, and the honest read. Commit as the chimes exploration
   doc + entry, wiki_retro either way.
